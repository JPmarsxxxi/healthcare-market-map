# Sources — checked and credible. Add here before reusing a search.

## US — digital health / market sizing
- **Rock Health** — https://rockhealth.com/insights — VC-grade, free, quarterly/biannual
  digital health funding + market reports. Latest checked (2026-09-11): US digital
  health startups raised $7.4B across 244 deals in H1 2026; capital concentrating in
  mega-deals ($100M+, ~half of all funding); mental health and weight-management lead,
  skewed heavily consumer-direct (64% B2C vs 29% average).

## UK — NHS structure and financing
- **The King's Fund** — https://www.kingsfund.org.uk — independent UK health policy
  think tank. Direct fetch blocked (403); reached via search instead. Confirmed:
  ~80% of NHS funding from general taxation/NI, 11.3% of GDP on health (2022),
  ~25% of NHS spend arguably reaching the private sector (uncleanly tracked). Also
  the source for the "Innovation, economic growth, medtech and the NHS" piece —
  MedTech Venture Kickstarter, the £100M MedTech/medicines start-up fund, King's
  Health Partners Ventures (NHS-backed fund).
- **Nuffield Trust** — https://www.nuffieldtrust.org.uk — independent, known
  specifically for forensic, non-partisan NHS finance analysis. **Fetches directly
  (unlike King's Fund) — the primary source for the deep scrub.** Two full reports
  read 2026-09-16:
  - "Where does the NHS money go?" (Gainsbury & Julian) —
    https://www.nuffieldtrust.org.uk/resource/where-does-the-nhs-money-go — real
    spending-growth-by-service figures, 2013/14-2023/24.
  - "How has the role of the private sector changed in UK health care?" (Dayan,
    Gainsbury & Bagri) —
    https://www.nuffieldtrust.org.uk/news-item/how-has-the-role-of-the-private-sector-changed-in-uk-health-care
    — private-sector spend growth, and the NI/Wales/Scotland/England self-pay
    disparity that's now logged in `gaps.md`.
- **NHS England itself** — https://www.england.nhs.uk/long-read/structure-of-the-nhs/
  — the primary-source structural explainer (ICSs, commissioners, providers). Fetched
  directly, confirmed structure: Government -> NHSE -> 7 Regional Teams -> 42 ICSs ->
  providers.

## Emerging markets / global
- **McKinsey Healthcare Systems & Services Insights** — https://healthcare.mckinsey.com
  — free published reports. Real examples worth reading directly: India's Aravind Eye
  Care System and Narayana Hrudayalaya — high-volume, radically cheaper care models
  that originated in a resource-constrained market, not the US/UK. Also: private-payor
  growth trends across East Asia/Middle East/North Africa as state systems open up.

## Pharma/biotech R&D segment — checked 2026-09-17, full six-step workup
- **One Nucleus** — https://onenucleus.com — Cambridge/London life-sciences network,
  500+ member organisations. His real, physical, local target pool.
- **BIA (BioIndustry Association)** — https://www.bioindustry.org — UK-wide, 600+
  members.
- **Capterra/G2 reviews of Benchling and Dotmatics** — real named user friction:
  Benchling's regulatory-submission support is weaker than its R&D story; Dotmatics'
  different modules integrate poorly.

  respectively. Alchemy confirmed NOT FDA-approved, pursuing 510(k), currently
  research-tool positioned.
- **Adaptyv, edison scientific, Domino Data Lab** — real companies hiring "forward
  deployed" engineers specifically for biotech/life-sciences R&D right now. Checked
  their customer bases directly: Domino's named customers are GSK/AstraZeneca;
  Adaptyv's are "top 5 pharma" and frontier AI labs — both enterprise-skewed, neither
  chasing small teams.
- **Scispot, Ardigen, DeepLife** — platform companies also selling into this space.
- **Epoch Biodesign** — real, funded, named UK biotech, hiring a Scientific Data
  Engineer right now for exactly this kind of pipeline work. Live target, not
  hypothetical.
- The manual-literature-curation-can't-keep-pace problem is stated repeatedly in the
  scientific literature itself (searched directly, multiple academic sources
  converge on this), not just an assumption.
