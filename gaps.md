# Gaps — the actual output of this whole project

Empty until a region or segment has been researched deeply enough to log something
real. A gap only goes here with: which region/segment it came from, why it looks real
(evidence, not a hunch), and whether it's B2B (consultancy) or B2C (product) shaped.

## Candidate gaps — real evidence behind each, not yet acted on

1. **Northern Ireland / Wales self-pay healthcare growth** — B2C-leaning, possibly
   B2B (a service selling into private providers there). Self-funded private care
   spend grew 218% (NI) and 124% (Wales) from Sept 2019-Sept 2023, vs just 20% in
   England. 10x+ England's rate. Source: Nuffield Trust, "How has the role of the
   private sector changed in UK health care?" (Dayan, Gainsbury, Bagri). Nothing else
   researched so far even looked below the England/UK-aggregate level — this gap
   exists partly *because* everyone else defaults to England. **Not yet explored:
   why** — could be NHS waiting-list severity, regulatory difference, income
   difference. Needs its own pass before acting on it.
2. **Dentistry — confirmed shrinking NHS coverage.** B2C or B2B (a service helping
   patients navigate/afford private dental, or a practice-support tool). Two
   independent Nuffield Trust reports converge on this: real-terms spend on
   community dentistry fell outright (funding report), and NHS dental coverage is
   shrinking (private-sector report). A real, evidenced, currently-underserved gap,
   not a hunch.
3. ⭐ **Small/under-resourced Cambridge-area biotech R&D teams — the strongest gap
   found so far, and it CONFIRMS the niche already recommended, not a new idea.**
   B2B. Full six-step workup in `segments/pharma-biotech.md`. Real, structural,
   admitted-in-the-literature problem (manual research-paper curation can't keep up
   with publication volume); real proof people already get paid to fix versions of
   it (Scoop, Alchemy, Adaptyv, edison scientific, Domino Data Lab all doing pieces
   of this); every one of those competitors chases big pharma only (GSK, AstraZeneca,
   "top 5 pharma") — nobody found is going after small teams specifically; a real,
   physically local pool of 500+ organisations (One Nucleus, Cambridge) to draw from.
   Named live target already sitting in his own job data: **Epoch Biodesign**,
   hiring for exactly this kind of data-pipeline work right now.
